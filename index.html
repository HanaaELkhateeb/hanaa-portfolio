01 · Featured Capstone & Industrial Telemetry

IADAM — AI-Based Industrial Pump Diagnostics & Predictive Maintenance

Physics-Grounded Digital Twin Telemetry (379,786 sensor records across 150 industrial pump trajectories)

🎯 Problem

Unexpected failures in critical industrial pumps (such as VVER-1200 nuclear cooling systems) cause costly downtime and severe operational hazards when relying solely on reactive or fixed-schedule maintenance.

🔭 Objective

Predict Remaining Useful Life (RUL) in hours, estimate real-time failure probability, diagnose specific fault types (e.g., seal_failure, bearing_wear, cavitation), and generate automated maintenance protocols.

⚙️ Methodology

Engineered 39 temporal and sensor features (vibration, temperature, power factor) across 379,786 telemetry rows. Built a multi-model pipeline: LSTM for RUL regression, Random Forest for failure probability & multi-class fault classification, Cox Proportional Hazards (Survival Analysis) for asset risk ranking, and a Gemini-powered RAG Agent linked to fault protocol JSON knowledge bases.

📈 Key Results

  • LSTM RUL Prediction: Achieved Test R² = 0.9998 with an MAE of 8.46 hours (RMSE: 11.12 hrs) across lifetimes up to 3,265 hours.
  • Survival Risk Ranking: Cox model achieved a Concordance Index (C-Index) of 0.810 across 150 pump assets.
  • Fleet Triage: Automatically flagged 45 high-risk pumps requiring immediate intervention (>60% failure probability) and triggered live diagnostic alerts with root-cause action plans.

02 · Business Analytics & Multi-Task ML

E-Commerce Retail — Product Tiering, Revenue & Demand Prediction

541,909 transactional records across 4,372 customers and 38 countries

🎯 Problem

E-commerce catalogs contain thousands of SKUs with extreme variance in order frequency, returns, and revenue contribution—making manual inventory prioritization and revenue forecasting inefficient.

🔭 Objective

Segment products into actionable revenue and volume tiers, predict product revenue classes, identify "Best Seller" high-volume items, and estimate transaction sales value.

⚙️ Methodology

Cleaned 541K+ records, flagged returns, and engineered behavioral features (SaleFrequency, NumProducts, UnitPrice, Quantity). Applied Quantile Binning & K-Means Clustering for segmentation, followed by Random Forest & XGBoost Classifiers and a Random Forest Regressor, wrapped in an interactive product lookup engine.

📈 Key Results

  • Best-Seller Volume Classification: Random Forest achieved 96.88% accuracy (F1 = 0.98 on high-volume tier).
  • Revenue Tier Classification: XGBoost reached 93.81% accuracy (outperforming baseline RF at 91.28%).
  • Sales Value Regression: Random Forest Regressor achieved R² = 0.7715 with an MAE of $0.52 per line item.

03 · Deep Learning & Medical Computer Vision

Automated Blood Cell Subtype Classification using Transfer Learning

Multi-class microscopy image classification across 4 leukocyte subtypes (224×224 RGB)

🎯 Problem

Manual microscopic identification and counting of white blood cell subtypes (Eosinophil, Lymphocyte, Monocyte, Neutrophil) is labor-intensive and prone to inter-observer variability in clinical labs.

🔭 Objective

Build and optimize an automated Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) pipeline capable of accurately classifying blood cell microscopy images into 4 diagnostic categories.

⚙️ Methodology

Applied Transfer Learning comparing VGG19 and ResNet50 backbones pretrained on ImageNet. Executed two-stage training: frozen feature extraction followed by selective fine-tuning (unfreezing top 4 layers with low learning rate Adam(1e-4)), paired with real-time Data Augmentation (rotation, width/height shifts, zoom) and L2 regularization to combat overfitting.

📈 Key Results

  • Architecture Optimization: Fine-tuning top convolutional blocks alongside Data Augmentation substantially reduced validation loss and prevented overfitting on limited medical samples.
  • Production Readiness: Exported trained model weights and built visual training/validation accuracy & loss diagnostics for clinical model auditing.
CNN Transfer Learning Pipeline4 Cell Classes · 224×224px
Engineering Takeaway: Combining selective layer unfreezing with geometric image augmentation and Dropout regularization is critical for generalizing CNNs on biomedical datasets.

04 · Temporal Analytics & Multi-Store Demand

Multi-Store Retail Time Series & Seasonality Exploration (2010–2018)

9 years of daily sales (230,090 records) across 7 stores and 10 product lines

🎯 Problem

Retail chains operating across multiple stores and product lines struggle to separate store-level structural performance from seasonal product demand fluctuations over multi-year horizons.

🔭 Objective

Decompose 9 years of daily unit sales (2010–2018) to identify top-performing stores, seasonal cycles, and product demand distributions for inventory allocation.

⚙️ Methodology

Parsed 230,090 daily timestamps (zero missing values; mean 780.9 units/day, range 238–1,205) and aggregated temporal trends at monthly and yearly granularities across all 7 stores and 10 products.

📈 Key Results

  • Store Performance Gap: Store 4 led overall volume with 32.02M units sold—nearly 2.8× higher than the lowest-performing Store 3 (11.32M units).
  • Product Balance & Seasonality: Product 7 led sales (19.15M units) vs. Product 8 (16.58M units), with strong recurring annual seasonality across all product lines.

05 · Robotics, Computer Vision & Embedded Hardware

Autonomous Minesweeper & Obstacle-Avoidance Robotic Vehicle

Multi-sensor fusion prototype integrating metal detection, computer vision edge perception, and ultrasonic navigation

🎯 Problem

Manual landmine clearance and hazardous terrain reconnaissance expose human operators to life-threatening risks and slow down safe area surveying.

🔭 Objective

Engineer an autonomous ground vehicle capable of scanning terrain for buried metallic hazards, detecting path boundaries/obstacles in real time, and navigating safely without human intervention.

⚙️ Methodology

Integrated a custom inductive metal detection circuit with an embedded microcontroller, ultrasonic distance sensors for dynamic obstacle avoidance, and OpenCV edge-detection algorithms to process real-time camera feeds for path and hazard localization.

📈 Key Results

  • Hardware-Software Integration: Delivered a working field-ready robotic prototype combining real-time sensor fusion with autonomous motor control.
  • Real-Time Hazard Alerting: Synchronized metallic anomaly triggers with path-halting and obstacle-rerouting logic.
